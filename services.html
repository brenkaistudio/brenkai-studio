What we do

Two things,
done
well.

We don't list ten services we half-offer. We do two, and we take both the whole way — from the first conversation to the site being live.

01

Websites & Landing Pages

Marketing sites, landing pages and portfolios, built from scratch for your business. We pick the simplest tool the project actually needs — often plain HTML and CSS, sometimes a framework when the content calls for it. Never a page builder, never a bought template.

Either way the page only ships what it actually needs, so it loads fast on a mid-range phone on mobile data — which is where most of your visitors are.

02

UI & UX Design

The design work that happens before any code: understanding who the page is for, what they came to do, and what has to be on screen for them to do it.

You get wireframes and a full visual design you can react to while changes are still cheap — not a surprise at the end. And because the same three people then build it, nothing gets lost in a handoff.

How we work

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We start with your business, not your website. What you sell, who buys it, and what you need this site to actually do. You leave this step with a written scope — so we both know what "done" means.

  2. 02

    Design

    Structure first, then visuals. You see the layout before it's decorated, and you review real screens rather than describing changes over email.

  3. 03

    Build

    We write the code by hand and test it on real screen sizes, with a keyboard, and with a screen reader. Performance and accessibility are checked as we go, not bolted on at the end.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We put it live, hand over everything, and walk you through how to update it. The files are yours — plain HTML and CSS, readable by any developer you hire next.
